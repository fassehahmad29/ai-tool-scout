Beginner Guide

Your first week with AI tools

One free tool a day builds the habit — and shows you what is actually worth paying for.

  1. Day 1–2: ChatGPT or Claude free plan — ask questions, summarise articles, draft emails.
  2. Day 3: Perplexity — research one real topic and check its cited sources.
  3. Day 4: NotebookLM — upload a PDF or lecture notes and ask questions about it.
  4. Day 5: Generate one image in ChatGPT, Gemini or Canva AI for a real post.
  5. Day 6–7: Repeat the task you do most. Only then decide if a $20 plan earns its money.
Prompt Guide

Write prompts that get better answers

Weak prompts get generic answers. Four additions fix most of them:

  1. Give a role: “Act as an experienced copywriter…”
  2. Give the audience: “…writing for busy parents in the UK.”
  3. Give the format: “Reply with a 5-point list, each under 15 words.”
  4. Give an example: paste one good result and say “match this style.” Then iterate — ask for a sharper second draft.
Money Guide

Make money with AI tools — honestly

AI does not print money; it cuts the cost of skilled work. The services people actually pay for:

  1. Content services: blog drafts, product descriptions and social posts for local businesses (Claude or ChatGPT + Grammarly polish).
  2. Design services: thumbnails, logos drafts and ad creatives (Canva AI, image generators).
  3. Video services: short-form edits and AI voiceovers for creators (Runway, ElevenLabs).
  4. Rule that keeps clients: AI drafts, you verify and finish. Sell the finished result, not “AI content.”
Buying Guide

Free vs paid: when is $20 worth it?

Every major chatbot has a genuinely useful free plan. Upgrade only when one of these is true:

  1. You hit free limits during paid work, not casual chats.
  2. You need a paid-only feature: Claude Code, deep research at volume, or higher image/video limits.
  3. The tool saves you one billable hour a month — at that point $20 is cheap.
  4. Otherwise stay free and stack free tools instead — see the $0 stack below.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free AI tool to start with?

ChatGPT's free plan for general use, Claude's free plan for long documents and writing, and Perplexity for research with sources. All three are free with no card required — the Tool Finder above matches one to your task.

ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini — which one should I pay for?

At roughly $20/month each: ChatGPT for the widest range of features, Claude for writing, long documents and coding (Claude Code is included in Pro), Gemini if your work lives in Gmail and Google Docs. Our head-to-head section breaks it down.

Are these prices the same worldwide?

No. Prices are shown in US dollars and some providers charge differently by country or through app stores, where taxes may be included. Claude Pro, for example, is $20/month billed monthly or $17/month billed yearly in the US. Always check the official pricing page for your country before subscribing.

Can AI tools really help me earn money?

They make skilled work faster — content writing, design, video editing and coding services are the proven routes. The tool is not the business: clients pay for verified, finished work. Our money guide lists the services readers actually sell.

How often is this page updated?

Reviews and prices on this page were checked on 12 November 2026. AI tools change pricing and features often, so confirm details on the official site — every comparison above links to the provider's own pricing page.
